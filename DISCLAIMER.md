## Hardware and data safety

Video processing is demanding: it can drive GPU/CPU usage, memory
consumption, and disk writes to their limits for long periods, and
hardware, drivers, and third-party runtimes can fail at any time. Keep
backups of your input files, use the default overwrite protection (a
completed output is never silently replaced), and monitor system
stability during long batch jobs. Processing time, output size, and
quality depend entirely on your hardware and the selected engines.
